# Third-party software

The ADA core is maintained separately from its generator backend.

- **RAEv2**: `https://github.com/nanovisionx/RAEv2`, pinned as a submodule at
  `8a0d238f8dc3b261aba98b217f6c79c0182e8e94`. Upstream and derivative
  integration code are governed by CC BY-NC 4.0.
- **DINOv2, SigLIP2, Qwen3-VL, Diffusers, PyTorch, and torchvision** are loaded
  as external dependencies or model assets and retain their respective terms.

No pretrained model weights or datasets are distributed in this repository.
